MULTIPLAYER ARENA

Every online player in one shared room (up to 12). Real-time movement sync, shared enemies, coins and flags. Rewards are flushed to the database every 20 seconds by the server.

SOLO RUN

A private instance. Enemy balls scale with your level, and everything you collect is yours. Perfect for learning the dash and farming flags.

1 VS 1 DUEL

Queue against another online player. First to 3 knockouts or highest score after 120 seconds. Winner takes 140 coins + 220 EXP, loser still earns.

👑

Bosses demand levels and flags. Each of the 8 bosses has unique attacks, HP, damage and a signature grant-only skin.
